Section 0

Before you start

Module D is 42 documents across five packs, all printed on one shared letterhead. Set the letterhead up first — every document inherits it, so a problem there shows up 42 times.

The foundation
One letterheadHeader, footer with page numbers, signature, seal, amount in words, watermarks
The packs
5 + 6 + 14 + 9 + 8Payroll · Attendance · Reports · HR Letters · Sales & Finance = 42
Documents read live data — so seed it first. A Salary Register for a month with no payroll is correctly empty, not broken. Before you start, make sure the business you are testing has: a payroll run, some attendance, an approved leave request, an invoice with a payment, and at least one employee with a salary structure. Section 12 lists exactly what each pack needs.

How to read the results

  1. Every document opens as a PDF. Preview in Browser opens it in a tab; Download PDF saves it.
  2. Check three things on each: the letterhead is right, the data is real (not placeholders), and the totals add up.
  3. Mark each case P, F or B. Marks are saved in this browser.
  4. Section 11 is a straight tick-list of all 42 — use it to confirm nothing is missing.

Suite 1 · 11 cases

Letterhead Foundation

Do this first. Everything downstream inherits it.

What the letterhead is made of. The header comes from your business record — legal name, address, phone, GSTIN, PAN. Only the footer line, signatory and images are set on the Letterhead screen. If the header is wrong, fix the business under Management → Businesses, not here.
Suite 2 · 7 cases

Amount in words

Indian numbering — lakh and crore. Check these with the exact figures given.

Why this matters. Every document here is an Indian statutory or commercial form. A salary certificate reading "one million two hundred thousand" is wrong on a Form 16 or a bank advice. It must read lakh and crore.
