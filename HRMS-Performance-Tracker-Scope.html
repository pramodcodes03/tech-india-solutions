Module A · Performance

Dashboards, KRA & KPI

The foundation of the module — what management sees at a glance, and the master data that everything else is scored against.

1 Admin Dashboard ADMIN · HR

  • Total Employees, Active KRAs and Active KPIs at a glance
  • Pending Goal Approvals and Completed Reviews counters
  • Average Performance Score across the organisation
  • Department-wise performance comparison chart
  • Top Performers and Low Performers lists
  • Upcoming Review Dates calendar

2 Employee Dashboard EMPLOYEE

  • My KRAs and My KPIs with live progress percentage
  • Achievement Score against assigned targets
  • Pending Reviews and Manager Feedback inbox
  • Performance Trend graph across past cycles
  • Rewards & Recognition earned

3 KRA Master — Key Result Areas ADMIN · HR

  • Fields: KRA Name, Department, Designation, Description, Weightage, Review Frequency, Manager, Status, Start Date, End Date
  • Review frequency options — Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, Yearly
  • Multiple KRAs per employee with a live weightage validator (must total 100%)
  • Example set-up: Sales Growth 40% · Customer Satisfaction 20% · Team Collaboration 15% · Learning & Development 15% · Attendance 10%

4 KPI Master — Key Performance Indicators ADMIN · HR

  • Fields: KPI Name, Related KRA, Measurement Unit, Target Value, Achieved Value, Weightage, Score Formula, Status
  • Measurement units — Number, Percentage, Currency, Hours, Rating
  • Multiple KPIs under a single KRA (e.g. Sales Growth → Monthly Revenue, New Customers, Repeat Customers, Conversion Rate)
  • Automatic score calculation from target vs achieved, per the configured formula

Assignment, Cycles & Assessments

5 Goal Assignment ADMIN · HR · MANAGER

  • Cascade assignment — Admin → Department → Designation → Employee
  • Direct assignment to one or many specific employees
  • Bulk assign a KRA set to an entire department or designation in one action
  • Copy goals forward from the previous cycle

6 Performance Cycle ADMIN · HR

  • Configurable cycles — Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, Yearly
  • Cycle open / lock control so scores cannot be edited after finalisation
  • Automatic cycle roll-over with review due dates

7 Approval Workflow

  • Escalation trail — Employee → Manager → HR → Admin
  • Each stage records who acted, when, and their remarks
  • Send-back-for-revision at any stage
  • Pending-at-my-desk queue for every role

8 Self Assessment EMPLOYEE

  • Employee enters Achievements, Challenges, Learnings, Future Goals and Comments
  • Evidence upload — PDF, PNG and JPG attachments against each KRA
  • Saved as draft until submitted; locked once it moves to the manager

9 Manager Assessment MANAGER

  • Rating on a 1–5 scale — 5 Outstanding, 4 Exceeds Expectations, 3 Meets Expectations, 2 Needs Improvement, 1 Poor
  • Feedback, suggestions and comments per KRA
  • Promotion recommendation and training recommendation flags

10 HR Review HR

  • Moderate manager ratings where they are out of line with the department
  • Verify performance against attendance, penalty and warning data already in the system
  • Finalise the score and generate the appraisal record

Module A · Performance (cont.)

Scoring, Rewards & Reports

11 Final Score Formula

  • Weighted calculation across all KRAs — (Sales × 40%) + (Attendance × 20%) + (Behaviour × 20%) + (Learning × 20%) = Overall Performance %
  • KPI scores roll up into their parent KRA automatically
  • Formula and weightages configurable per department without code changes

12 Performance Bands & Bell Curve

  • Score-to-band mapping — 95–100 Outstanding · 85–94 Excellent · 75–84 Very Good · 60–74 Good · 40–59 Needs Improvement · Below 40 Unsatisfactory
  • Optional bell-curve distribution — Outstanding 10% · Excellent 20% · Good 50% · Average 15% · Poor 5%
  • Band thresholds editable by HR

13 Rewards — Auto Recommendation

  • System suggests Salary Increment, Bonus, Promotion, Training or PIP based on the final band
  • Recommendations feed directly into your existing appraisal and increment records
  • HR can accept, override or reject each recommendation

14 Reports — with PDF & Excel export

15 Notifications

  • Goal Assigned · Goal Updated · Goal Approved · Self-Assessment Due · Review Due
  • Review Completed · Manager Feedback Available · Appraisal Generated · Promotion Recommended
  • Delivered through your existing in-app and email notification system, permission-gated per role

16 Role-Wise Permissions

  • Admin — Create KRA/KPI, Assign Goals, Final Approval, Full Reports
  • HR — Create KRA/KPI, Assign Goals, HR Review, Full Reports

Module B · Trackers

Three Operational Trackers

Three record-keeping registers with role-based access. Every one of them ships with an analytics view, Excel import and export, and Monthly / Date-wise / Yearly filters.

1 Break Sheet Tracker

  • Employee Name — dropdown that auto-fetches the Employee ID
  • Out Time and In Time — calendar and time picker
  • Total Timing — calculated automatically from out and in time
  • Break Type and Remarks
  • Analytics — average break duration per employee, per department, longest breaks, daily totals

2 Diesel Tracker

  • Serial Number / Code, Month, Date, Bill Number, Slip Number, Time
  • Quantity and Amount with running totals
  • Upload option for the receipt or slip — PDF or image — so every entry is backed by proof
  • Monthly budget tracking overview — allocated vs consumed vs remaining
  • Analytics — consumption trend, cost per litre movement, month-on-month comparison

3 Daily Visitor Tracker

  • Date of Visit, Candidate / Visitor Name, Mobile Number
  • Source — dynamic dropdown, HR can add new sources at any time
  • Arrival Time and Purpose of Visit — dynamic dropdown
  • Called By, Interview By and Availability Status
  • Analytics — visitors by source, by purpose, by day, interview conversion view
Included with every tracker. Role-based access so only designated users see each register · Excel import for historical data · Excel and PDF export · Monthly, date-wise and yearly filters · search and sort on every column.

Module C · Enhancements

System Modifications

Targeted changes to the running system. Three items are chargeable; three more from your list are already built and are shown here so the record is complete.

To be built

1 Recent Activity Log — Super Admin Masking NEW

  • Actions performed by the Super Admin display as “System Admin” in the Recent Activities log
  • The existence of the Super Admin role is hidden from regular admins throughout the interface
  • The real identity stays intact in the underlying audit record for compliance

2 Combined Leave NEW

  • Employees can combine fractional balances across leave types in a single request — for example 0.5 Casual + 0.5 Sick = one full day
  • One request, one approval, correctly deducted from both balances
  • Approver sees the split before approving; payroll picks it up correctly

3 Leave Balance Gate NEW

  • An employee with no paid leave balance is blocked from submitting a leave request
  • Clear message on the form showing the available balance per leave type
  • Currently the system allows over-balance requests and lets HR decide the paid / unpaid split — this changes that behaviour
One decision needed from the client. Blocking leave when the balance is zero also stops an employee from applying for genuine Leave Without Pay — for example a medical emergency after balances are exhausted. We will build this as an on / off setting so HR can allow an LWP request as an exception. Please confirm whether the block should be absolute or allow an LWP exception.

Already built & live

4 Budget Addition Trail DELIVERED

  • Mid-month top-ups are recorded with the exact sanction date, amount, note and the admin who added them
  • The budget total increases from that date forward instead of appearing as if allocated on the 1st

5 Budget Edit & Delete DELIVERED

  • Authorised users can edit or delete a budget entry to correct a mistyped amount
  • Individual top-up entries can also be removed

6 Record Payment — Receipt Upload DELIVERED

  • Upload field beside Reference / UTR No. on the Record Payment screen
  • Accepts PDF, JPG and PNG up to 5 MB — bank slip, cheque scan or UPI screenshot
  • Receipt viewable from the payment list, detail and edit screens
